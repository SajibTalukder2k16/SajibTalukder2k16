## 🚀 About Me

I'm a **PhD student in Computer Engineering at Virginia Tech** (Innovation Campus, Alexandria, VA), working as a Graduate Research Assistant and Graduate Teaching Assistant. My research sits at the intersection of **cellular network security, wireless simulation, and AI**. Before my PhD, I earned an M.S. from The George Washington University and spent two years as a software engineer building Bangladesh Railway's national ticketing platform.

- 🔬 **Research focus**: Cellular & wireless security, SIM-farm fraud detection, digital twins, IoT & systems security, multi-agent AI
- 📡 **Tools I build with**: ns-3, 5G-LENA, OMNeT++, Python, C++, PyTorch, FastAPI, Linux
- 👨‍🏫 **Teaching**: GTA for ECE 4524, Artificial Intelligence and Engineering Applications (Fall 2026)
- 🤝 **Collaboration**: Open to research collaborations and Summer 2027 internships

## 🔭 What I'm Working On

- **SIM-farm digital twin**: an ns-3 / 5G-LENA emulation of SIM-box fraud built on 123K+ real U.S. LTE towers and 80K+ crowdsourced measurements, validated with major U.S. mobile operators
- **Multi-agent fraud detection**: dedicated ML agents for network, timing, behavioral, location, and identity features, fused into one fraud score with gateway localization
- **Explainable decisions**: fine-tuning a small language model to turn detection outputs into analyst-ready explanations
- **SigCap measurement research** with the University of Notre Dame: how device motion relates to radio features
